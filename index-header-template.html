About

I am a postdoctoral researcher jointly affiliated with the Department of Statistics at the University of California, Berkeley, and the International Computer Science Institute, working with Michael Mahoney. I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from the University of California San Diego, where I was advised by Ioana Dumitriu and Todd Kemp.

Research

  • Deep learning theory: optimization, generalization, scaling, and spectral structure.

  • Randomized numerical linear algebra: matrix functions, sketching, and low-precision computation for large models.

  • Random matrix theory: local laws, phase transitions, and free probability.
