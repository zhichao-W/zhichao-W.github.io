I am a postdoctoral researcher jointly affiliated with the [Department of Statistics](https://statistics.berkeley.edu/) at the University of California, Berkeley, and the [International Computer Science Institute](https://www.icsi.berkeley.edu/), working with [Michael Mahoney](https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~mmahoney/index.html). I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from the University of California San Diego, where I was advised by [Ioana Dumitriu](https://math.ucsd.edu/people/profiles/ioana-dumitriu) and [Todd Kemp](https://mathweb.ucsd.edu/~tkemp/).

My research develops the mathematical and computational foundations of artificial intelligence, using tools from high-dimensional probability and numerical linear algebra. Current interests:

- Deep learning theory and the foundations of AI
- Randomized numerical linear algebra and optimization for large language models
- Random matrix theory
