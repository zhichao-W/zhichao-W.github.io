About me

I am a fourth-year PhD student in the Department of Mathematics at UC San Diego. I am very fortunate to be advised by Ioana Dumitriu and Todd Kemp. Prior to UCSD, I received my M.Sc. degree in Mathematics from Texas A&M University, supervised by Michael Anshelevich, and B.Sc. degree in Mathematics from Beihang University.

Research Interests

My current research focuses on theoretically understanding and improving fundamental algorithms in data science, especially in deep learning, using tools from high-dimensional probability.

  • Random Matrix Theory and Free Probability Theory

  • Deep Learning Theory

  • High-dimensional Statistics
